# BricoWerx Redesign Notes

## What Moved
- The **Waitlist** form was moved from immediately under the hero to the end of the page (Band C), acting as the primary conversion goal after the reader understands the product. A sticky nav CTA and a hero CTA serve as shortcuts to it.
- **What is a piece** and **Why the summaries matter** were merged into a single section with a unified file tree and a 4-level selector (Card, Surface, Summary, Full Source).
- The **Bricolage etymology** section was collapsed into a single FAQ item.
- The **Piece Kinds** section was condensed into a single horizontal row of status chips (shipping, next, later).

## What Was Cut
- **Chapter numbers (00-09)** were removed to reduce the "homework" feel and allow the reader to jump around freely.
- The **"Three rules of digital bricolage"** were removed to keep the focus on the product and its concrete value to engineering teams.
- **Pause Film / Play Teaser** were completely removed as requested, since there was no actual 45-second teaser video available.
- **Instrument Serif** and the decorative quotes were dropped, tightening the typographic scale to just Space Grotesk, Inter, and JetBrains Mono.
- **Extra colors** (Plum, Ochre, Olive) were removed. The color system was simplified to Terracotta (Reuse), Sage (Verified), and Slate (AI).

## Copy Changes
- The Hero copy was entirely rewritten to clearly state the category ("BricoWerx is a command-line tool...") as requested by the brief and subsequent user feedback.
- The Before/After section copy was simplified to emphasize token counts and review time.
- The "Knowledge nobody can find" trio was compressed into one-sentence cards.
- The FAQ was pruned down to just the 6 requested questions.
